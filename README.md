One emoji for the dream vacation: 🗿 — the moai of Easter Island (Rapa Nui).

Why the moai: one glyph names a single faraway place, a stone face staring out
to sea — a postcard you can read at a glance, with no words at all.

`main.ts` writes that single emoji to `/output/entry.txt`, inside the 1–8 character gate.
